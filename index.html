Power electronics

Every power chip must get rid of its heat.

In this study, a chip smaller than a postage stamp turns about 100 watts into heat. That heat has to cross a thin, soft thermal pad to reach the heat sink.

Too hot, and the chip slows down, ages faster or cracks.

Real 3-D CAD model ANSYS temperature, section through the chip
The usual way

Datasheets rank pads by one headline number.

But heat must also jump across two contact surfaces, and the pad changes thickness when it is clamped. The headline number ignores both.

The key insight

How well a pad touches matters as much as what it is made of.
